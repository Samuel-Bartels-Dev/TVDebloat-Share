# LG OLED65C9PUA — operator observations

## Current evidence — October 30, 2026

The rooted-TV investigation established firmware 03.50.31 and webOS SDK/release
4.5.0. Hearth 1.2.0 was installed and the operator confirmed apps, HDMI and
Home operation. The C9 Home-ribbon helper, wedding-photo native screensaver,
Hylia clock and Home-only notes card have scoped evidence in
[the maintained model observations](../../../docs/lg-c9-hearth-observations.md).
That document records final selected behavior, recovery and verification gaps.
These were separately approved rooted-TV customizations, not native TVDebloat
write capability. The native adapter remains read-only. No reusable executable
profile is promoted from these observations.
